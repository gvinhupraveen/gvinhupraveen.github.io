Automatic Tapping Machine

  • Custom machine that automates a repetitive tapping operation
  • Servo-controlled linear motion of the tapping head, with a pneumatic ejection cylinder
  • Less manual handling and a shorter cycle time
CompanyAvinya Engineering Solutions, Pune
TypeClient project
Timeline2025
My roleDesign and manufacturing
ToolsSolidWorks, Fusion 360, VMC machining, 3D printing

Overview

The client had a tapping step that was done by hand, part after part. The brief was to automate it so an operator no longer had to feed, tap and remove each component. I designed the machine, produced its models and drawings, and manufactured and assembled it.

How It Works

  • Servo-driven tapping head: a servo drives the tapping head along a linear axis, for controlled feed into the part and a repeatable stroke.
  • Pneumatic ejection: a pneumatic cylinder pushes the finished part out at the end of each cycle, ready for the next one.
  • Modular frame: an aluminium extrusion frame keeps the machine rigid and easy to adjust during commissioning.
  • Custom brackets and plates: designed in SolidWorks and Fusion 360, with 2D drawings for manufacturing.

Build

Outcome

The machine cut out manual intervention in the tapping step and reduced cycle time for the client's repetitive operation.
